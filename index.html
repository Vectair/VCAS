Welcome to VCAS

VCAS shows you nearby aircraft while you drive, plotted by bearing and distance so you know where to actually look — plus turn-by-turn navigation to get you somewhere. Two things, one screen.

Three views

RAW A TCAS/ND-style instrument display — no map, just the traffic picture. Your default view.
AIR Top-down airspace view, every tracked aircraft, unfiltered.
HYBRID Road map with traffic overlaid.

Tap any aircraft icon or indicator for its callsign, type, altitude and more. In RAW, tap the range readout (top right of the display) to cycle through how far out it shows.

3D View

Tap 3D (only available while stationary or moving slowly) and point your phone at the sky — aircraft in the direction you're pointing appear on screen, planetarium-style. Tap one for details.

Getting somewhere

Tap 📍 to search a destination by name, or tap the map directly — VCAS routes you there and keeps tracking traffic the whole way.

What the symbols mean

Shape and colour show how easy an aircraft should actually be to spot with your own eyes right now — not how close it is on the map.

A dashed outline means an aircraft is about to enter view. An upward chevron means it's almost directly overhead — look up.
